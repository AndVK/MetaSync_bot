MetaSync Bot

Simple Crypto Price Tracker in Telegram

Track Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and Solana (SOL) prices without leaving Telegram. Daily reports and instant alerts — no signup, no clutter.

Open MetaSync Bot in Telegram →

What the bot does

  • Daily reports — every day at 12:00 UTC you receive current prices and 24h changes for BTC, ETH and SOL.
  • Volatility alerts — instant notification when any tracked coin moves more than 1%.
  • On-demand prices — send /price at any time to see current rates.
  • Per-coin alert toggles — configure which coins trigger alerts via /change.

Why MetaSync Bot?

Most crypto bots are overloaded with charts, portfolios and paid tiers. MetaSync Bot does one thing well: it keeps you posted on BTC, ETH and SOL prices with zero friction.

  • No signup — works with your existing Telegram account.
  • No wallet access, no email, no personal data required.
  • Free and open source.

  1. Open the bot: @MetaSync_bot

Frequently asked questions

Is MetaSync Bot free?

Yes. The bot is completely free — no paid tiers, no in-app purchases, no ads.

Which cryptocurrencies are supported?

Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) and Solana (SOL).

How accurate are the prices?

Prices are fetched from a public market-data aggregator and refreshed every minute, so quotes are close to live market values.

Will the bot spam me?

No. You receive one daily summary, plus an alert only when a tracked coin moves more than 1%. You can disable alerts per coin anytime with /change, or unsubscribe completely with /stop.

Does the bot store personal data?

Only your Telegram chat ID, which is needed to deliver messages. No wallets, no emails, no profile information.
